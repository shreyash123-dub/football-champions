# ⚽ Football Champions 2026

A simple browser-based football game. Choose your club, pick a star player, and play matches against different opponents.

## How to Play

1. Choose your **Club**
2. Choose your **Star Player** (higher rating = better chance to score)
3. Choose your **Opponent**
4. Click **START MATCH**
5. Use **Shoot**, **Dribble**, or **Pass** for 6 rounds
6. Earn coins based on the result

## Features

- 5 clubs including Bengaluru FC
- 6 star players (Messi, Ronaldo, Neymar, Mbappé, Haaland, Sunil Chhetri)
- Career stats (Wins, Losses, Coins)
- Clean dark theme with pitch animation

## How to Run

Just open `index.html` in any browser.  
No installation needed.

## Play Online (GitHub Pages)

1. Go to repo **Settings → Pages**
